# Design/Style Guide

* I want the aesthetic to feel like "Vladimir Nobokov's office--with peripherals."
  * Typewriter fonts
  * Dates that look like they were stamped
  * Loan lists that look like loan cards from back in the day
  * Pictures of card catalogs/stacks of books
* I also want it to match the vibe of the house.
  * Jewel tones
  * Pops of gold
  * Plants and greenery
* Another thought
  * Menu that looks like the drawer to a card catalog on the left. Center of the page, brass.
  * Light glints on it when you hover over it.
  * Drawer casts a shadow over the page
  * The interface itself should feel like it contains a piece of furniture.
* More thoughts
  * Collections page could be the fronts of several drawers to a card catalog
  * The interface should not attempt to eliminate all evidence that it is digital.
  * Modern interactions should remain responsive and intuitive, but visual elements should borrow their physical
    metaphors from a private library.

### Circulation

I want this to be an exercise in minimalism

A background of one of the colors on the palette with a floating window in the center.
Scan the book.
It checks the ISBN/QR code against the catalog and determines if it's coming or going.

On a checkout - prompt for borrower
On a checkin - prompt for rating.

*note - eventually we will need to build in "user ratings" and "aggregate ratings", but i'm not worried about that on v1"

I would also LOVE it if the system would generate an email to the borrower (obviously we prompt for that, as well).
The email would autosend and have a copy of the borrowing policy in it. This would act as a digital record for the borrower in case they forget.

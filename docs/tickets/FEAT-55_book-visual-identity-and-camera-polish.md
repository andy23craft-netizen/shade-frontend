# FEAT-55 -- Book Visual Identity and Camera Polish

**Status:** Ready, pending visual approval.

**Depends on:** Existing Reading Room identity, camera ISBN capture, shared controls, and tenant identity boundaries.

## Objective

Unify book surfaces around a restrained private-library identity and bring camera capture chrome into the same accessible system.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Book browse/detail/intake/circulation/discovery share intentional tokens, textures, hierarchy, and states without changing navigation semantics.
- [ ] Seasonal atmosphere is decorative, bounded, reduced-motion safe, and cannot leak tenant-specific identity.
- [ ] Camera capture uses the approved wood-brown surround and shared buttons while preserving a large unobscured decode region.
- [ ] Permission prompt guidance, denied/unavailable camera, decode failure, retry, manual ISBN fallback, focus, and screen-reader announcements are complete.
- [ ] Empty states may add concise personality copy while keeping the next action and functional meaning explicit.
- [ ] 320px, 200% zoom, forced colors, contrast, reduced motion, slow assets, camera performance, and lazy-loading budgets pass.

## Out of scope

Per-owner identity packages, selectable skins, navigation redesign, and new scanner behavior.

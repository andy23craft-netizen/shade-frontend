## Aesthetics

On the books page, the persistent controls behave strangely. They start to scroll until the header disappears and then they freeze. I want them to just stay frozen the whole time. It draws your eye to it as it moves up a few pixels and then stops. There's plenty of room on that side of the screen for it to just stay still the whole time. 
Also, when entering selection mode, the two combined boxes are too big and you have to scroll within the controls. We don't want that. we need to size the selection box down a bit to fit on the screen better.
